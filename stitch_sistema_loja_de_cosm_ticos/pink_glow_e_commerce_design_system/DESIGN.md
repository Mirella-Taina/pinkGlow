## Brand & Style

This design system expresses modern luxury, radiant warmth, and sensory delight for an upscale cosmetics and makeup brand. It translates the functional wireframe blueprints—ranging from customer touchpoints (hero showcase, product catalogs, cart, checkout) to admin dashboards and operational forms—into an editorial, tactile, and conversion-optimized retail experience.

The design language balances high-fashion editorial aesthetics with ergonomic e-commerce utility:
- **Luminous Minimalist Luxury:** Generous breathing room, velvety surfaces, soft rose glows, and warm nude backdrops instead of clinical stark whites.
- **Audience & Tone:** Modern beauty enthusiasts, makeup professionals, and conscious consumers seeking quality, sophistication, and effortless shopping. The interface conveys intimacy, self-care, and professional trust.
- **Visual Personality:** Sculpted, pill-curved interactive elements, ambient champagne-tinted illumination, high-contrast editorial typography paired with ultra-legible modern sans for pricing, metadata, and data tables.

## Colors

The palette is tuned for cosmetics merchandising, rendering skin tones, textures, and packaging with warm fidelity:

- **Primary (`#DB2777` - Deep Rose Glow):** Used for primary conversion triggers ("Finalizar Compra", "Adicionar à Sacola", key CTAs), active tabs, and primary action buttons across storefront and admin.
- **Secondary (`#D4AF37` / `#E5C483` - Champagne & Warm Gold):** Reserved for prestige accents, rating stars, loyalty badges, exclusive product badges, and refined border rings.
- **Tertiary (`#F472B6` / `#9D174D`):** `#F472B6` delivers luminous interactive hover states and tags, while `#9D174D` (Berry Plum) provides deep, high-contrast headings, admin section identifiers, and focused states.
- **Surface & Backgrounds:**
  - Base canvas: `#FFFBFB` (Alabaster Warm White) with subtle tinted undertones.
  - Surface cards and containers: `#FFFFFF` and `#FDF2F4` (Blush Porcelain).
  - Subtle borders and dividers: `#F8E8EC` and translucent rose borders `rgba(219, 39, 119, 0.08)`.
- **Neutrals & Text:**
  - On-surface display: `#1F1518` (Espresso Plum) ensuring crisp accessibility without the harshness of pure `#000000`.
  - Body and secondary copy: `#4A1D2F` and `#70535E` for soft, elegant legibility.

## Typography

The dual-type pairing addresses the tension between high-glamour beauty magazines and fast conversion commerce:

- **Editorial Headlines (`Playfair Display`):** Applied to the hero statement ("Venha realçar sua beleza!"), brand logos, showcase titles, section headers, and modal titles. Conveys high-fashion prestige, authority, and warmth.
- **Interface & Transactional Layer (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Built for UI legibility. Used for catalog listings, SKU codes, administrative tables, search inputs ("Base Bruna Tavares"), currency pricing, and form field inputs. It remains crisp across dense dashboard matrices and small mobile screens.
- **Letter Spacing:** Headlines utilize slightly tightened tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) for an intimate, editorial presence, while all micro-labels, tags, and category chips utilize open tracking (`+0.04em` to `+0.06em`) for effortless scanability.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout supports both customer exploration (fluid, breathing galleries) and administrative workflows (structured data grids shown in the admin wireframes):

- **Grid Configuration:**
  - **Desktop (>=1024px):** 12-column grid, max-width `1280px` centered canvas, `gutter: 1.5rem` (`24px`), `margin: 2rem` (`32px`). Catalog grids display 4 or 5 columns as modeled in the `relatorio.php` / catalog wireframe.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column grid, `gutter: 1.25rem`, `margin: 1.5rem`. Products reflow to 3 columns.
  - **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column layout, `gutter: 1rem`, `margin: 1rem`. Catalogs reflow to 2 columns with full-width bottom actions.
- **Spacing Rhythm:** Standard 8pt linear rhythm. Form inputs and button vertical paddings align to `space-sm` (`8px`) or `space-md` (`16px`). Section vertical offsets use `space-xl` (`40px`) to prevent clutter between hero carousels and category chips.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy reflects the "Pink & Glow" moniker—subtle, candle-lit ambient halos rather than heavy, harsh drop shadows:

- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** Neutral `#FFFBFB` base, no shadow.
- **Level 1 (Card & Shelf Default):** 
  - `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(219, 39, 119, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(74, 29, 47, 0.03)`
  - Outlined with a hair-thin perimeter `1px solid rgba(248, 232, 236, 0.8)`. Used for catalog product cards, admin action panels, and cart summary containers.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Active Product):**
  - `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(219, 39, 119, 0.12), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.1)`
  - Elevated slightly (-3px translateY) on hover with a champagne or pink edge transition.
- **Level 3 (Modals, PIX QR Code & Cart Floating Panels):**
  - `box-shadow: 0 20px 48px -8px rgba(31, 21, 24, 0.15), 0 0 1px 1px rgba(219, 39, 119, 0.1)`
  - Accompanied by a frosted glass backdrop filter (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px); background-color: rgba(31, 21, 24, 0.35)`).

## Shapes

The design system adopts a soft, ergonomic pill-and-curve geometry consistent with cosmetic packaging (compact cases, lipstick tubes, and skincare vials):

- **Buttons & Filter Chips:** High roundedness (`border-radius: 9999px` / pill-shaped) for interactive friendliness and touch compliance.
- **Product & Content Cards:** `rounded-2xl` (`1rem` / `16px`), providing soft containers for product photography and detail listings.
- **Inputs & Search Bars:** `rounded-xl` (`0.75rem` / `12px`), creating clear inset troughs for text entry.
- **Thumbnails & Media Containers:** Softened with `rounded-xl` to harmonize with surrounding card containers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Conversion):** Pill-shaped, gradient or solid fill `#DB2777` with white text, font `Plus Jakarta Sans 600`. Subtle glow shadow on hover (`box-shadow: 0 4px 16px rgba(219, 39, 119, 0.35)`).
- **Secondary / Outline:** Pill-shaped, `1.5px` border in `#DB2777` or `#D4AF37`, transparent background, text color `#9D174D`.
- **Administrative Destructive Button:** (e.g., "Apagar cliente", "Deletar produto", "Excluir" in order tables): Subtle blush background (`#FDF2F4`), crimson text (`#BE123C`), transitioning to solid `#BE123C` with white text upon confirmation prompt.

### Product Cards (Catalog / Vitrine)
- Encased in a `rounded-2xl` surface with `#FFFFFF` background and subtle `#F8E8EC` border.
- Generous padding (`space-md`). Top section houses 1:1 aspect ratio product image on warm nude `#FAF5F6` backdrop.
- Product label in `body-sm`, title in `title-md`, followed by high-contrast price tag in `#DB2777` (`price-display`).
- Quick-add button appears inline or full-width at card bottom.

### Filter & Category Chips
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padded `space-xs` vertically and `space-md` horizontally (e.g., "blush", "rímel", "contorno").
- Default: `#FDF2F4` surface with `#70535E` text. Active state: `#DB2777` fill with crisp white text.

### Search & Form Fields
- Inset `rounded-xl` with `#FFFFFF` or `#FAF5F6` background and border in `rgba(219, 39, 119, 0.2)`.
- Focus state: `border-color: #DB2777` paired with an ambient focus ring (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(244, 114, 182, 0.25)`).
- Clear floating placeholder labels in `#70535E`.

### Cart & Checkout Modules
- **Cart Item Component:** Horizontal row inside a `rounded-xl` card containing product thumbnail, quantity stepper, variant selector, and delete trigger.
- **PIX / Payment Container:** Prominent, centered `rounded-2xl` modal or card with high elevation, clean QR display placeholder, one-click copyable payload input, and gold-accented status indicator.

### Administrative Action Lists
- Form panels (as sketched in `pagina de admin`, `create`, `update`, `apaga produto`) feature structured single-column form fields with clear group headings, consistent full-width input heights (`48px`), and pill-shaped confirmation buttons aligned to the right or center.
